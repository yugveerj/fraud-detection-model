Summary of results

Isotonic-calibrated LightGBM, validated temporally; the operating point is frozen on validation and reported on a held-out future slice. Objective: decision quality and validation rigor — not leaderboard AUC.

The decision

A calibrated probability meets a frozen, dollar-optimized review threshold, drawn below as a control limit. Select a transaction — where it lands is the decision.
