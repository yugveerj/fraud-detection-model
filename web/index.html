Summary of results

Isotonic-calibrated LightGBM, validated temporally; the operating point is frozen on validation and reported on a held-out future slice. The objective is decision quality and validation rigor — not leaderboard AUC.

The decision

A calibrated probability meets a frozen, dollar-optimized review threshold. Select a transaction — where it lands against the threshold is the decision.
